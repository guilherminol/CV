# Factored Talent Interview — Brief (source of truth)

## Situation
Guilhermino was invited to a **Factored Talent Interview** (recruiter screen, via Zoom).
Factored: AI/ML/Data staffing company co-founded by Dr. Andrew Ng, builds elite AI/ML/Data
teams for U.S. clients. English fluency is an explicit evaluation pillar.

## What the email asks him to prepare and share

1. **Elevator Pitch (2 mins):** name, current role, brief academic + professional
   background, years of experience, summary of key strengths (top skills, tools,
   industries, areas of expertise).
2. **One project with the CAR framework (3 mins):** a specific data/AI/ML/engineering
   project. Focus: problem + business context; responsibilities, tools, methods;
   business/user impact.
3. **General technical questions** to gauge understanding of core concepts.

## Deck rubric (from "2025 - Candidates Soft-Skills Prep", full text in
factored-soft-skills-deck.txt — read it)

- 5 pillars evaluated: Technical, Coding, Business Acumen, Communication, English.
- Elevator pitch: MAX 2 minutes; structure Quick intro → Summarized background →
  Main tech-stack → 1 project example → Motivation; use numbers/data for business
  impact; confident delivery, eye contact with camera, hand gestures.
- CAR: C = Context (~30s): business problem, situation, stakeholders, how he
  communicated progress. A = Action (~1.5 min): responsibilities, techniques/tools,
  deadlines/priorities, initiatives proposed. R = Result (~1 min): validated results,
  business impact (net revenue, users, time/cost reduction). Deck says "present it in
  5 minutes"; the email says 3 minutes — target the email's 3 minutes.
- English metrics: listening (paraphrase questions back), vocabulary ("I" + powerful
  verbs: led, implemented, resolved, managed), fluency (calm pace), grammar (tenses,
  connectors).
- Business acumen: big picture, business impact, alignment with business needs; more
  senior = more business contribution expected.

## Constraints for the prep materials

- **Language: English** (interview is in English). Spoken scripts must be
  speakable-aloud prose with word counts and a calm-pace timing estimate
  (~130–150 words/min).
- **100% grounded in content/profile.md** — every fact, number, and claim must trace
  to the profile. No invented metrics, no embellishment beyond what is there. Honest
  attribution (team framing where profile says "the team delivered…", "I" for direct
  work — deck explicitly asks to use "I" for his own actions; keep it honest).
- Delivery aids: mark natural pause points, anticipated follow-up questions with
  suggested answers, and pronunciation/stress notes only where genuinely useful.
- Numbers matter to this interviewer — surface the strongest profile metrics
  (e.g., ~90% analysis-time reduction, 30s vs 1 day, 68.1% organic adoption, 1,446
  specialist hours eliminated, R$4,750 MRR in 45 days, ~93% lead-gen cost savings,
  90-table redesign with 70% performance gain) where they fit naturally.
- English background is a differentiator worth one line: former English instructor +
  15-person global team across India/UK (multilingual agile environment).
