harnessing the power of data and cutting-edge algorithms to drive tangible business results.

schedule maintenance, minimize downtime by 20%, and save millions in repair costs.
My main motivation lies in my passion for applying advanced analytics and machine learning 
techniques to drive innovation, improve decision-making, and positively transform businesses 
and society as a whole.
That’s why in my spare time,  I have completed 3 courses recently about Advanced Machine 
Learning, Computer Vision and Machine Learning with Python: From Linear Models to Deep 
Learning while acting as a volunteer mentor.

It includes having a fundamental understanding of 
how a business operates, then using that knowledge 
effectively to make logical business decisions.

technical skills, and soft skills, used to positively 
impact an organization in beneﬁt of career growth

CAR Framework: Example For a DE Project
C: Context
XYZ Corporation faced critical 
roadblocks with its ETL process, 
causing delays in reporting and 
scalability issues. Manual 
processes resulted in bottlenecks, 
impacting data reliability and 
hindering business growth.
A: Action
As the lead data engineer, I initiated a 
comprehensive assessment. Recognizing the 
need for a scalable solution, I redesigned the 
ETL architecture using Apache Spark, enabling 
parallel processing and optimized partitioning. 
Simultaneously, I introduced stringent data 
quality measures and automated workﬂows 
with Apache Airﬂow.
R: Result
The ETL process time was cut by 40%, 
overcoming delays. Scalability 
improvements allowed seamless handling 
of a 50% increase in data volume. Data 
quality enhancements reduced errors by 
20%, ensuring more accurate insights. 
Automation led to a 30% cut in 
infrastructure costs.

4. English Pillar
--- SLIDE 17 ---
Listening
Make sure you accurately understood the interviewer’s 
message by paraphrasing the question (repeating the 
idea with your words) or asking a follow up question.
Vocabulary
Use “I” when describing your actions. Expand your 
vocabulary when explaining your professional 
experience by incorporating powerful verbs such as: 
“I strongly contributed, managed, led, implemented, 
resolved”.
Fluency
Maintain a calm speaking pace and pay attention to 
your pronunciation.
Grammar
Ensure you follow sentence structure while correctly 
using verb tenses and connectors.
